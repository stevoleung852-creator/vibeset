How it works

From idea to a finished home screen in about a minute.

1

Type a vibe

Describe the look you want in a few words. Vibeset can expand it into a fuller art direction for you.

2

Tap Generate

You get a wallpaper, 20 app icons and square and wide widgets, all designed together so they match.

3

Make it yours

Save the wallpaper, add the widgets, and set your new icons with Shortcuts. The app walks you through each step.
