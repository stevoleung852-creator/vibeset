Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy explains how Vibeset AI: Icons & Widgets ("Vibeset", "the app", "we", "us") handles your information. Vibeset is made by Stephen Leung, an independent developer based in Australia. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

The short version

  • Vibeset has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or location.
  • To make your sets, we send the themes you type, and any images you choose to add, to AI services that generate the images.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

What we collect

Please don't type personal or sensitive information, such as names, addresses or health details, into your prompts.

Photos

If you choose to save images, Vibeset asks for permission to add photos to your library. It doesn't read your photo library. If you attach a reference image, only that image is sent.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers we need to run Vibeset:

  • Higgsfield receives your prompts and any reference images in order to generate the images in your sets.
  • Anthropic (Claude) receives your prompts so it can expand them into a fuller art direction before images are made.
  • RevenueCat receives your anonymous app ID and purchase information so it can manage your subscription and set packs.
  • Apple processes all payments under its own privacy policy.
  • Our hosting provider stores our server data and logs.

These AI services are used only to create your content. We send them your prompt text and images, never your app ID or purchase details. Each provider handles data under its own terms and privacy policy. Some of these providers are based outside Australia, including in the United States, so your information may be processed overseas.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or the service.

We don't sell or rent your information. We don't use it for advertising, and we don't use third-party analytics or advertising tracking.

How long we keep it

We keep your sets, prompts and credit balance while you use Vibeset so the app keeps working. Server logs are kept only briefly. Purchase records may be kept longer where needed for tax or legal reasons. We delete or anonymise data that we no longer need.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: Email us and we'll delete your sets, prompts and credit data from our servers. Because there are no accounts, please tell us roughly when you made your sets and a few of the themes you typed so we can find them.
  • Access or correct your data: You can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it.
  • Remove the app: Deleting Vibeset removes the images stored on your device. The anonymous ID in your Keychain may stay on the device so your purchases still work if you reinstall.
  • Subscriptions: Manage or cancel them in your iPhone's Settings under your Apple ID.

If you are in the EU, UK or another region with similar laws, you may also have the right to object to or restrict how we use your data, and to complain to your local data protection authority. In Australia you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au). We'd appreciate the chance to sort things out first, so please email us.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and the keys for our AI providers are kept on our server, never in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

Vibeset isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If the changes are significant, we'll let you know in the app.
